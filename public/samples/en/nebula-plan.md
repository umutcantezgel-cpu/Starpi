# Project Nebula: project plan

> Sample document for the Starpi demo. The company, the people and all figures are fictitious.

## Summary

Project Nebula replaces the route planning system of Helio Freight with a self-hosted planner. The public launch of Project Nebula is planned for 12 May 2027. Lena Park leads development; Tom Becker is the product owner.

## Budget

The approved budget for Project Nebula is 480,000 EUR. It is split into 310,000 EUR for development, 95,000 EUR for infrastructure and 75,000 EUR for training and rollout. The steering group approved the budget on 3 March 2026.

## Milestones

- Kickoff: 14 September 2026
- Beta at the Hamburg depot: 1 February 2027
- Public launch: 12 May 2027

## Team and meetings

The core team has 9 people: 5 developers, 2 data engineers, 1 designer and 1 product owner. The steering group meets every Tuesday at 10:00.

## Targets

Nebula should cut empty truck kilometres by 12 % in the first year and bring the average planning time per route below 90 seconds.
